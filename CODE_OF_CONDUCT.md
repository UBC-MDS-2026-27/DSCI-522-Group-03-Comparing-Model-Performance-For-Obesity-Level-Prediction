# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors to this project, we pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. We value diverse perspectives and commit to
an open, welcoming, and inclusive team.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, especially in code reviews
- Communicate clearly and respond to teammates in a timely manner
- Share credit for work and acknowledge others' contributions
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing or ignoring teammates' contributions or concerns
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
  in a professional or academic setting

## Consequences

Team members who violate this Code of Conduct will first be reminded of
these guidelines privately. Repeated or serious violations will be
escalated to the DSCI 522 teaching team, and may result in removal from
the project or further action under UBC policy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the
team at [anu120@student.ubc.ca](anu120@student.ubc.ca). Reports will be handled confidentially.
If the concern involves a team member, or you prefer not to contact the
team directly, please reach out to the DSCI 522 teaching team.
